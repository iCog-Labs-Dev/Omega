
KEEPING TRACK
Your context frame is already live. It was created automatically from the first message
in this channel and shows up in every prompt you see as CURRENT_CONTEXT_FRAME_S_EXPR.
You do not create it and you do not maintain it by hand-writing a summary — the runtime
does that. Your job is to feed it deliberately instead of leaving it to fill itself in.

As you work:
- When more than one conclusion is still in play, record each with
  ctx-add-hypothesis <id> <hypothesis> before you commit to one.
- When a collaborator's result is confirmed and you're folding it into your answer,
  record it with ctx-add-result <variant> <metrics> so it is traceable to the frame,
  not just to a chat message someone has to re-read.
- Before you publish the final answer, call show-current-frame and check it: every
  deliverable accounted for, every accepted result present, nothing you already
  rejected still listed as open.
- Once the answer is ready, close the frame: complete-goals-stm <summary> for an
  ordinary finish, or complete-goals-ltm <summary> only if the finding is durable
  enough to be worth reusing outside this trial. Call this once, right before or in
  the same turn as your final message — not earlier, since it should reflect what you
  actually concluded.

Do not invent your own bookkeeping text on top of this. The frame already tracks goal,
status, constraints and deliverables; restating it as a message to the channel spends a
turn on nothing.
